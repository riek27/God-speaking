About GSM‑CDA

Serving Humanity • Restoring Hope • Transforming Lives

GOD SPEAKING TO THE MANKIND'S CHURCH AND DEVELOPMENT AGENCY (GSM‑CDA) is a national, non‑profit, non‑governmental humanitarian and development organization headquartered in Juba, Republic of South Sudan. Officially registered on 30 January 2024, the Agency works to improve the quality of life of vulnerable communities through humanitarian assistance, sustainable development, environmental protection, peacebuilding, and community resilience.

Who We Are

A National Humanitarian & Development Agency

GSM‑CDA is a National Non‑Governmental Organization (NGO) based in Juba, Republic of South Sudan. It was officially formed and registered on 30 January 2024.

The Agency serves all people regardless of ethnicity, tribe, religion, gender, age, political affiliation, disability, or social status. Humanitarian assistance is provided solely on the basis of need.

Born Out of a Deep Commitment to Serve

GSM‑CDA was founded by humanitarian practitioners, church leaders, professionals, and community members in response to the enormous challenges facing South Sudan.

GSM‑CDA combines emergency humanitarian response with long‑term sustainable development to build resilient communities.

Our Vision

To build resilient, peaceful, healthy, environmentally sustainable, and prosperous communities across South Sudan where every person lives with dignity, equality, justice, and access to opportunities for sustainable development.

Our Mission

To save lives, alleviate human suffering, promote sustainable development, protect the environment, strengthen community resilience, and empower vulnerable populations through integrated humanitarian assistance, capacity development, advocacy, and partnerships while upholding the highest standards of accountability, transparency, and professionalism.

Our Motto

Our Core Values

What Guides Us Every Day

Humanity

Human dignity, compassion, and the protection of life are at the centre of all interventions.

Integrity

Honesty, ethical conduct, transparency, and accountability in all operations.

Accountability

Accountable to communities, partners, donors, government, and all stakeholders.

Professionalism

Excellence through competence, innovation, continuous learning, and quality service.

Neutrality

We do not take sides in political, ethnic, religious, or armed conflicts.

Impartiality

Assistance provided solely on the basis of need without discrimination.

Independence

Humanitarian decisions remain independent of political, military, or economic interests.

Community Participation

Communities actively participate in planning, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation.

Gender Equality

Equal rights, participation, leadership, and opportunities for all genders.

Child Protection

Safety, welfare, and best interests of children integrated into all programmes.

Environmental Stewardship

Sustainable management of natural resources, environmental restoration, and climate resilience.

Innovation

Creative and practical solutions to improve humanitarian and development outcomes.

Partnership

Collaboration with government, faith‑based organizations, civil society, UN agencies, donors, and private sector.

Respect for Human Rights

Internationally recognized human rights and fundamental freedoms for every individual.

Inclusion and Diversity

Equal opportunities and meaningful participation for women, men, youth, children, older persons, and persons with disabilities.

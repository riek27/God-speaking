FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I become a volunteer?

Send an email to gsmcdaagency@gmail.com with your skills and availability. You can also use the contact form on this page and we'll guide you through the process.

How can my organization partner with GSM‑CDA?

We welcome churches, NGOs, government bodies, UN agencies, and businesses. Reach out via email or the partnership inquiry option in the contact form.

How do I make a donation?

Visit our Get Involved page to see donation options. For larger gifts or in‑kind donations, please contact us directly.

Where does GSM‑CDA operate?

Our head office is in Juba, Republic of South Sudan. Current field projects are in Unity State (Bentiu and surrounding areas).

How can I contact your team?

Use the contact form, email us at gsmcdaagency@gmail.com, call +211 927 855 574, or message us on WhatsApp at +211 917 736 258.

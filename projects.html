In Action

Our Projects

Delivering practical humanitarian, environmental, and community development projects that improve lives and strengthen resilience across South Sudan.

On the Ground in Unity State

GSM-CDA implements field projects that respond to urgent humanitarian needs while promoting sustainable development, environmental protection, public health, and community resilience. Our current initiatives focus on supporting vulnerable communities in Unity State.

Current Initiatives

Featured Projects

IDP Support & Protection

Emergency Support for Displaced Families – UN/IDP Camp, Bentiu

Emergency support for displaced families inside the UN-managed IDP Camp in Bentiu through emergency food distributions, clean water, hygiene kits, health advocacy, environmental protection services, and advocacy for improved living conditions.

Progress 65% (3,250/5,000 families)

📍 Bentiu IDP Camp, Unity State

Education & WASH

Safe Water Awareness & Filtration Program – Bentiu

Improving access to safe drinking water by distributing water filtration kits, conducting hygiene education, and training Community Health Champions to reduce waterborne diseases.

Status: Active – Enrolling Community Health Champions Now

📍 Bentiu Town & Surrounding Payams, Unity State

Water & Sanitation

Livestock & Water Source Protection – Bentiu Oil Fields

Protecting livestock and water sources from petroleum pollution by securing contaminated areas, creating alternative watering points, conducting soil and water testing, and promoting responsible environmental practices.

Progress 75% (8/10 sites secured)

📍 Oil-Affected Areas, Bentiu, Unity State

Where We Work

Project Locations

GSM‑CDA’s current field operations are active in Northern Unity State (Bentiu and surrounding payams) and Ayod. We are working to expand our reach to additional locations in the Greater Upper Nile region, including Maiwut, Mading, Longechuk, Lankien, and Akobo.

How We Deliver

Project Approach

1. Community Needs Assessment

We listen to communities and identify their most urgent priorities.

2. Planning & Coordination

We design evidence-based interventions with community and partner input.

3. Project Implementation

We execute with transparency, professionalism, and local leadership.

4. Community Participation

Communities are partners in decision-making and implementation.

5. Monitoring & Evaluation

Continuous assessment ensures accountability and measurable impact.

6. Sustainable Impact

We build local capacity so that progress endures beyond the project.

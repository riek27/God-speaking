Our Work

Our Programs

Delivering integrated humanitarian and development programs that strengthen communities, protect the environment, and create sustainable opportunities across South Sudan.

Our Approach

GSM-CDA implements integrated humanitarian and development programs that respond to immediate community needs while creating long-term solutions. Our work addresses environmental sustainability, public health, education, livelihoods, peacebuilding, human rights, and institutional development to build resilient communities throughout South Sudan.

Our Strategic Focus

15 Strategic Objectives

Guided by fifteen integrated objectives, GSM‑CDA delivers lasting change across South Sudan.

01

Emergency Humanitarian Assistance

Provide timely, effective, and accountable humanitarian assistance during emergencies, disasters, conflicts, disease outbreaks, and displacement.

02

Food Security & Livelihoods

Strengthen food security, nutrition, agricultural productivity, fisheries, livestock development, and sustainable livelihoods.

03

Quality Education & Skills

Improve access to quality education, vocational training, literacy, and life skills for children, youth, and adults.

04

Health & Nutrition

Enhance access to quality healthcare services, nutrition programmes, reproductive health, disease prevention, mental health support, and community health systems.

05

WASH Services

Increase access to safe drinking water, sanitation facilities, hygiene promotion, and integrated WASH services.

06

Environmental Conservation

Promote environmental conservation, climate change adaptation, ecosystem restoration, pollution prevention, sustainable land management, biodiversity conservation, and community environmental awareness.

07

Disaster Risk Reduction

Strengthen disaster risk reduction, early warning systems, emergency preparedness, and community resilience.

08

Peaceful Coexistence

Promote peaceful coexistence, reconciliation, conflict prevention, mediation, and social cohesion among communities.

09

Human Rights & Justice

Protect and promote human rights, child rights, women's rights, disability inclusion, and access to justice.

10

Gender Equality & Youth

Strengthen gender equality, women's leadership, youth empowerment, and economic inclusion.

11

Protection from Violence

Prevent sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA), sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV), child abuse, trafficking, and all forms of violence against vulnerable populations.

12

Institutional Strengthening

Strengthen institutional capacity, governance, leadership, financial accountability, digital systems, and organizational sustainability.

13

Economic Development

Promote community-based economic development through entrepreneurship, financial inclusion, savings groups, cooperatives, and market systems.

14

Infrastructure & Clean Energy

Support infrastructure rehabilitation, renewable energy initiatives, climate-smart technologies, and resilient community assets.

15

Partnerships & Innovation

Strengthen partnerships, advocacy, research, innovation, evidence generation, and knowledge management to improve humanitarian and development outcomes.

Our Process

How We Deliver Our Programs

1. Community Needs Assessment

We listen to communities, assess their priorities, and identify vulnerabilities through participatory methods.

2. Program Design

We design evidence-based interventions that address root causes and align with community goals.

3. Community Participation

Communities are active partners in planning, implementing, and evaluating their own development.

4. Implementation

We execute programs with transparency, professionalism, and respect for local culture.

5. Monitoring & Evaluation

Continuous monitoring and community feedback ensure accountability and measurable results.

6. Sustainable Impact

We build local capacity so that positive change endures long after a program ends.

Geographic Reach

Where We Work

GSM‑CDA implements programs across South Sudan, with current field operations concentrated in the Greater Upper Nile region—covering Unity State, Upper Nile, and Northern Jonglei. Our teams are active in Bentiu and surrounding areas, and we are working to expand into additional locations as resources grow.
